# What this hosted tool does not cover

Unofficial; not legal advice. Every answer already carries that disclaimer.

## What you can ask

- Indiana Code sections as of a date (structured snapshots 2017–2026).
- Official IGA companion PDFs as far as IGA published them (session laws,
  non-code, constitutions, historical tables).
- Current statewide court rules and posted county local-rule compilations.
- The electronic Indiana Administrative Code edition IAR labels Current.
- Indiana appellate clusters, with opinion bodies when those sources
  supplied them. That is not every Indiana decision ever issued.

## Not this tool

- Legal advice, citator status, “good law,” or KeyCite-style signals.
- A green “certified” mark on Court of Appeals cases. Recent slips may
  carry a caution. Confirm certification on MyCase. We do not certify.
- An official publication. Verify at [iga.in.gov](https://iga.in.gov/laws/ic/downloads)
  and the issuing court.
- The IGA legislative API. This project does not use `api.iga.in.gov`.
- Paper Administrative Code editions before the electronic IAR years.
- Proposed legislation that is not yet enacted in a loaded Acts volume.
- Confidential research. Do not paste sealed or client secrets into a
  public endpoint.
